# Working Agreement

How we work on this project. Read this at the start of every session.

## Direction and pacing
- **Context is not a go-ahead.** Background, goals and ideas you share are information. Build only the step you explicitly ask for. When unsure, ask before building.
- **Order of work:** data audit first (timestamps, rolls, coverage, aggressor-side quality). Strategy research only after you've seen the audit and said to start.
- **Lesson from session 1 (2026-10-12):** strategy rules, a backtester and two extra hypotheses (H04/H05) were built before you asked. They are **parked**, not deleted. See [[Session Log]].

## Research standards (from the brief)
- No ICT concepts.
- Quantitatively rigorous and candid. Challenge assumptions; don't agree just to be agreeable.
- Separate observed facts from model-derived estimates (especially dealer positioning and GEX).
- Chronological dev / validation / untouched test. Never retune on later periods and call it out-of-sample.
- Realistic costs, fills and latency. Report null and negative results plainly. A robust "doesn't work" counts as a success.
- Prefer affordable data. Verify current pricing, history, fields and licensing before recommending a purchase.

## The vault
- The Obsidian vault at `D:\Trading Bot` is the project's memory and research record. **Everything worth remembering goes in here**: goals, decisions, data facts, vendor research, ideas, results, mistakes and lessons.
- One note per individual research item (hypothesis, experiment, data source, method).
- Each working session gets an entry in [[Session Log]].
- The vault is also a git repo. Notes and code are committed; market data and vendor data never are.
